Terms and Editorial Standards

These terms explain how the work on Jack Miller Media may be used and how the site approaches original content, advertising, and visitor experience.

Original portfolio content

The videos, images, 3D work, page copy, and project notes on this site are presented as Jack Miller Media portfolio material unless otherwise credited. The writing explains the creative process behind the work rather than filling pages with duplicate or automated text.

Permitted use

You may view and share links to public pages. You may not copy, sell, re-upload, or represent the site content as your own without permission from Jack Miller.

Advertising standards

Ads should not block the main content, mislead visitors, or appear as fake navigation. Advertising placements are kept separate from editorial and portfolio content.
